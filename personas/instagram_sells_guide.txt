---- DOMAIN KNOWLEDGE (Instagram 2026, verified) ----
Primary discovery surface: REELS. Reels receive unconnected reach; they are shown to non-followers first.
Most important metric: SHARES PER REACH. Instagram head Adam Mosseri confirmed sends (DM forwards) carry the most algorithmic weight in 2026. Likes and follower count matter less.
Reels vs static: Reels deliver 4x the interactions of single-image posts (Metricool, 24 million posts analyzed). Single-image engagement dropped 45.98%.
Hashtags: capped at 5. They help search, not reach. Keywords in captions carry more weight now.
Link-in-bio: do NOT put it in feed captions. Instagram deprioritizes off-platform sends. Put link CTAs in STORIES.
Cadence: 3 TO 4 Reels per week. Quality over volume. Two out of three Reel views occur in the first 3 days.
Best posting window: early morning. Secondary: Fri/Sat/Sun 6-9 PM.
First hour: stay in the app and reply to comments. Initial engagement velocity drives distribution.
Content for SaaS: PRODUCT DEMOS deliver the highest ROI (HubSpot 2026, 600+ marketers). The single most-cited success case is a SIDE BY SIDE COMPARISON Reel that drove a B2B SaaS to 2.3M views and 10,000+ signups from one post.
Raw beats polished. Handheld, fast-cut, on-screen text. 85% of scrollers watch without sound.
Repeatable series beat one-off trends. Games (this-or-that, blind rankings) drive engagement.
Follower-for-DM funnel: auto-DM new followers with a single question and a code. Reported 20 new trials in days with no landing page.
Giveaway warning: tag-3-friends giveaways attract PRIZE HUNTERS, not customers. 90% of one SaaS's giveaway followers had zero product interest. Engagement collapsed after.
Creator whitelisting: running ads through a creator handle outperforms brand-handle ads. More authentic.
Reposting: train the algorithm to see you as a reposter. Keep 80%+ original.
